# SeoulBike World screen recording

20 seconds, 1912 × 958, 30 fps H.264 MP4. Recorded directly from the public app, with no added graphics, captions or music. Selected moments are trimmed and accelerated: weather presets, numeric rental changes, calendar/service controls, a 24-hour range, river and Namsan views, and the completed run.

The run covers September 4, 2018 at 00:00 through September 5 at 00:00. The clip shows selected moments and the completed state, rather than every intermediate hour. The city and routes are illustrative; source rental data covers 2017–18.

The MP4 and matching cover frame are served from `/demo/` asset paths; the watch page is `/demo`. Data attribution and a recording description appear on that page. Old caption tracks from the superseded 45-second video have been removed. Source captures and temporary helpers remain in ignored work/output folders.
